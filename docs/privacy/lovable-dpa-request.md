**Subject:** Data Processing Agreement for a Free-plan workspace using Lovable Cloud and the AI Gateway

Hello Lovable Privacy Team,

I run a small, non-commercial beta web app ("ONboard", project ID c20eb88c-629a-4925-8d88-db7bae006f4a)
built and hosted with Lovable. I am based in Germany and act as the controller for the personal data of
the app's end users. The app uses Lovable hosting, Lovable Cloud (database, authentication incl. managed
Google sign-in, file storage, edge functions) and the Lovable AI Gateway.

My workspace is currently on the Free plan. Your DPA page states that the DPA is included for Business and
Enterprise plans. Could you please clarify:

1. Is a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR available for Free or Pro workspaces, and if so, how
   can I conclude it? If not, which plan is required?
2. Where can I access the current list of sub-processors that apply to my workspace (hosting, Lovable
   Cloud, AI Gateway), including their locations?
3. For AI Gateway requests from a published app on a non-Enterprise plan: which model providers and
   regions process the requests, and which transfer mechanism (e.g. EU Standard Contractual Clauses or
   the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) applies to transfers outside the EEA?
4. What are the retention periods for Lovable Cloud logs (edge function, auth, database, storage) and for
   database backups, and can end-user data be removed from them on request?
5. Is data from my published app or its end users used to train or improve AI models on the Free plan, and
   can this be switched off?
